Persuade mode for school administrators. Extend the established public marketing family at /#/for-schools and preserve its brand, navigation and local artwork. The owner confirmed staff roles, classes, pupil cards, assignments and reports as the content, with “Sign up your school” as the primary action. No pricing, customer proof or institutional verification claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give schools a visible starting point and an honest path from an individual adult account to a school-owned workspace, not another teacher-only dead end.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Fredoka headlines, system reading type, violet actions, white/cloud ground, teal detail and approved island scenery. No new visual identity or generated assets.

STORY: Find For schools in the shared header, understand administrator/teacher/pupil access, then create and verify an adult account before explicitly creating the school. Existing adults sign in; invited staff use their invitation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared one-line desktop header, two-line school headline, concise explanation and visible signup/sign-in actions in the existing illustrated page-heading composition. Mobile uses the incumbent navigation disclosure and stacked content; actions wrap without clipping.

FORM: Precisely requested extension of the existing marketing-page family, code-led, no concept seed or new comp. Role/access definitions, ordered setup guidance, factual feature rows and native FAQ disclosures. Signup intent persists in allowlisted school-role query links through verification/sign-in/MFA; it never grants school access.
