# School administration — account surface extension

Operate mode. Extend the existing illustrated adult account shell, not the landing or learning UI. Teachers may have a personal workspace and school memberships concurrently. No existing workspace or pupil is moved implicitly. Invitations use email-bound, expiring, one-use links that an administrator shares privately; delivery by email is not claimed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make ownership unmistakable before an adult changes school access; refuse a merged list of personal and school classes.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the white account workspace, pale-purple sidebar, violet actions, Fredoka headings, system controls and existing island artwork. No new assets or global tokens.

STORY: Select a workspace, see its scope, invite staff, assign classes and revoke access without losing pupil work. Explain permission changes before submitting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the labelled workspace selector in the incumbent sidebar; the main column names the school and current role, followed by staff and a primary invitation action. On mobile, navigation and selector stack above a single content column; dense tables scroll inside labelled regions.

FORM: Precisely scoped extension of the approved account shell; code-led, no concept seed or new comp. Native forms/details, visible confirmation for sensitive changes, readable empty/loading/error states. Class archive/restore remains in the existing classroom screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
