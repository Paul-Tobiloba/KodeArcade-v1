# Byte Lab scene raster provenance

Generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool in generate mode, one call per scene, on 17 October 2026. Every call used `transparent_background: false`, with no reference-image arguments. The existing `public/images/meadow-board.png` was visually inspected for palette and style only. These are original fictional game backdrops, not photographs of real places.

Delivery: 768 × 768 opaque optimized PNG, Sharp compression level 9 with a 256-color palette. Impeccable uses sidecars for WebP, so PNG is used to keep the exact prompt inside every shipping raster. Exact generation prompts are embedded into each delivered image with Impeccable `embed-prompt` and recovered with `--read`. Original built-in PNGs remain at their generated paths. The central open area supports moving sprites; scene details frame the edges and upper third.

Verification: all eight final PNGs decoded at 768 × 768 with no alpha channel. Every embedded PNG tEXt prompt was read back and compared exactly with the tool-call prompt, including its original newline structure. Square and central 16:9 contact sheets were visually inspected for recognizable settings, open playable centers and absence of people, animals, vehicles, writing and watermarks. QA contact sheets are retained under ignored `output/byte-lab-scenes/`. The eight final files total 1,759,379 bytes. Experimental WebP versions and their sidecar are recoverably archived under `output/byte-lab-scenes/unused-webp/`, outside the shipping folder.
