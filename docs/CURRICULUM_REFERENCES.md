[Revised Grades 1–6 curriculum map, 20 October 2026](CURRICULUM_V3_GRADE_MAP_2026_10_20.md) proposes differentiated grade outcomes, earlier events, meaningful non-route projects, practical assessment and separate practice/mastery rewards.
